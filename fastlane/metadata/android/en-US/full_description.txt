Klover is an unofficial Android app for people who run a <a href="https://www.kavitareader.com/">Kavita</a> server and want a reader that feels at home on their phone. You need your own Kavita server to use it.

<b>Readers</b>
<ul>
<li>Manga, comics and webtoons, with tap zones, zoom and double-tap, left-to-right or right-to-left</li>
<li>A native EPUB reader (no web view) that follows much of the book's own CSS and draws tables and SVG pictures</li>
<li>PDF with sharp zoom</li>
<li>Read aloud for books: it keeps going with the screen off and shows as a media control, with the cover, on the lock screen and in quick settings</li>
</ul>

<b>Made for foldables</b>
<ul>
<li>Two pages at once when unfolded, and pages that turn like paper when held half open like a book</li>
<li>Tabletop mode, and layouts that keep clear of the fold</li>
</ul>

<b>Offline and in sync</b>
<ul>
<li>Downloads for whole series, with Wi-Fi only, pause and resume, and delete after reading</li>
<li>Your progress goes back to Kavita, also when you read offline</li>
</ul>

<b>Reading stats</b>
<ul>
<li>A "time left" estimate that learns how fast you read each kind of content</li>
<li>Streaks, a weekly goal and a clover garden that grows as you finish chapters</li>
<li>Backup: export and import your streak, stats, bookmarks, settings and profile look</li>
</ul>

<b>Yours</b>
<ul>
<li>Several accounts on one device, Material 3 Expressive design, wallpaper colours or your own, true black</li>
<li>Seasonal decorations around holidays, and a switch to turn them off</li>
</ul>

<b>Private</b>
There are no analytics, no ads and no crash reporting. Your sign-in is kept encrypted with the Android Keystore. The app talks to your Kavita server, plus an optional lookup of chapter names in MangaDex's public catalogue that you can switch off.

Klover is not affiliated with Kavita. It was made with the help of an AI coding assistant; the README says how.
